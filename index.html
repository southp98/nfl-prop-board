How picks are made

Each prop starts from the fair market chance: every book's over and under prices with the vig removed. We move it 25% toward what the player's 2026 game log says, using a recency-weighted bell curve plus his hit rate against this exact line. Edge is our chance minus the chance implied by the best available price.

Picks need at least 4 games, odds between −200 and +200, and 2+ points of edge. We leave a prop out when the player is listed Out or Doubtful, when the line sits far from his average, or when the books price him far from his history. Those gaps usually mean news the log can't see, like a role change or a teammate returning. Each player gets at most 2 picks and each market at most 5.
